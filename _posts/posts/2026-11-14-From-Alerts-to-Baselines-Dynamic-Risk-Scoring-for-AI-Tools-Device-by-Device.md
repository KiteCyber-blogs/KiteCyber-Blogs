# From Alerts to Baselines: Dynamic Risk Scoring for AI Tools, Device by Device

![From Alerts to Baselines: Dynamic Risk Scoring for AI Tools, Device by Device]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/linkedin_agent-runtime-you-dont-control-1024x535.jpg)

AI tools and agents are becoming an increasingly important part of business operations, but monitoring their activities requires more than traditional security alerts. Organizations need behavioral baselines that establish what normal activity looks like for a specific tool, device, and user. By analyzing factors such as device posture, file and process activity, network behavior, data classification, and user context, security teams can identify unusual patterns and potential threats more effectively. A gradual response strategy, ranging from monitoring and user warnings to approval requirements and blocking high-risk actions, helps maintain security without disrupting legitimate work.
