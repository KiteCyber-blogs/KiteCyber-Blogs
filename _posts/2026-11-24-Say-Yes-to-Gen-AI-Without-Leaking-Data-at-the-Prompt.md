# Say Yes to Gen AI Without Leaking Data at the Prompt

![Say Yes to Gen AI Without Leaking Data at the Prompt]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/Say-Yes-to-Gen-AI-Without-Leaking-Data-at-the-Prompt.png)

Generative AI tools have become part of everyday business workflows, but their growing use has created new data security risks for organizations. Employees frequently paste customer records, contracts, financial information, and source code into AI tools using personal accounts, often without realizing that sensitive company data may be exposed. Traditional Data Loss Prevention (DLP) solutions struggle to detect these activities because AI interactions happen through browser prompts, desktop applications, coding assistants, and clipboard transfers. Shadow AI incidents are increasing, making it essential for businesses to gain visibility into unauthorized AI applications and prevent sensitive information from leaving company devices. Kitecyber addresses these challenges through endpoint-based AI security and Data Shield capabilities that discover AI applications, classify sensitive information using context-aware detection, and enforce allow, warn, or block policies during paste and upload activities. Its lightweight agent provides protection across browsers and desktop applications while keeping full prompt contents private. By combining AI application discovery, on-device data classification, and real-time enforcement, organizations can maintain employee productivity while reducing data leakage risks and strengthening compliance.
