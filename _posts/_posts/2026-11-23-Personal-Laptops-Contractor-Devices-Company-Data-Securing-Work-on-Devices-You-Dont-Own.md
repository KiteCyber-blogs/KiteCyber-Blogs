# Personal Laptops, Contractor Devices, Company Data: Securing Work on Devices You Don't Own

![Personal Laptops, Contractor Devices, Company Data: Securing Work on Devices You Don't Own]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/kcblog2.png)

Businesses increasingly rely on contractors, partners, and employees using personal devices, creating new security risks for sensitive company data. Unmanaged laptops often lack proper security controls, making them vulnerable to credential theft, data breaches, and unauthorized access. The growing use of personal AI tools further increases these risks, as confidential information can be shared through applications such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini without IT visibility. Traditional VPNs, browser-based security, and full-device management often fail to provide adequate protection while maintaining user privacy. A modern BYOD strategy requires device verification, zero-trust access, separation of personal and corporate data, and endpoint-level data protection. Organizations must also control copy/paste, file uploads, USB transfers, and sensitive data shared with AI applications. Kitecyber addresses these challenges through Infra Shield, Device Shield, and Data Shield, combining secure access, device management, and unified data loss prevention through one lightweight agent. Its centralized platform helps businesses enforce security policies, monitor data movement, simplify contractor onboarding and offboarding, and maintain compliance with standards such as HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2, and ISO 27001. By adopting these measures, SMEs can protect sensitive information without slowing down business operations or compromising personal device privacy.
