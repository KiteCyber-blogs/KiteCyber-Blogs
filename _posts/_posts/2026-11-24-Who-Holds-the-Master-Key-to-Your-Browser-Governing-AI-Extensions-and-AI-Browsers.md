# Who Holds the Master Key to Your Browser? Governing AI Extensions and AI Browsers

![Who Holds the Master Key to Your Browser? Governing AI Extensions and AI Browsers]({{ site.baseurl }}/KC-blog1.png)

AI browser extensions, AI assistants, and agentic browsers are becoming increasingly common in workplaces, helping employees improve productivity and automate everyday tasks. However, many of these tools request extensive permissions to access browser tabs, emails, CRM platforms, HR systems, and other sensitive business information. Without proper monitoring, they can expose confidential data through prompts, uploads, and background activity. Traditional security approaches, such as URL blocklists and network monitoring, often fail to identify which applications are accessing sensitive information or how data is being shared. Organizations need a modern security strategy that provides visibility into AI applications, browser extensions, user activity, and data movement. Kitecyber addresses these challenges through AI application discovery, extension monitoring, endpoint-based Data Loss Prevention (DLP), and context-aware data protection. Its endpoint-first security approach helps businesses identify unauthorized AI tools, manage approved applications, and prevent sensitive information from leaving company devices without restricting legitimate AI adoption. Ultimately, securing AI in the workplace requires continuous visibility, controlled access, and proactive data protection.
