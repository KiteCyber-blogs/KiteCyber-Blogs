# The Agent Was Retired. Its Keys Weren't: Offboarding AI Agents Starts on the Device

![The Agent Was Retired. Its Keys Weren't: Offboarding AI Agents Starts on the Device]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/agent-retired-keys-werent-hero-1536x864.jpg)

AI agents are increasingly being adopted by organizations to automate tasks, access business systems, and improve productivity. However, when employees leave or AI agents are retired, organizations often overlook the credentials, cached tokens, MCP server registrations, scheduled tasks, memory files, and sensitive data left behind on employee devices. Simply deleting an AI agent does not guarantee that its access or background activities have been removed. As AI adoption grows, with some organizations managing more agents than employees, proper AI agent offboarding has become an important security requirement. Businesses need a structured approach that includes identifying installed agents, revoking credentials, verifying their removal from endpoints, clearing stored instructions and memory, and maintaining activity records. Kitecyber addresses these challenges through endpoint-based visibility into AI agents, running processes, device activity, credentials, and sensitive data movement. Its unified security approach helps organizations identify leftover agent activity, monitor unauthorized access, and prevent sensitive information from leaving company devices. Ultimately, effective AI agent offboarding requires more than revoking credentials; organizations must ensure that no hidden processes, permissions, or data access remain after an agent or employee leaves.
