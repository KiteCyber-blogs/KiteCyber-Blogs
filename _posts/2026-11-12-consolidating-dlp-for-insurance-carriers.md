# Consolidating DLP for Insurance Carriers: Replacing Point Tools With One Endpoint Agent Ahead of an Audit

![Consolidating DLP for Insurance Carriers](../images/Consolidating-DLP-for-Insurance-Carriers.png)

Insurance companies must protect sensitive policyholder information while meeting NAIC Model 668 requirements for data security, risk assessments, third-party oversight, and regulatory reporting. A unified endpoint-based [Data Loss Prevention (DLP) solution](https://www.kitecyber.com/product/data-loss-prevention-solution-vendor/) helps enforce consistent security policies across files, emails, browsers, USB devices, and GenAI tools. By detecting and blocking unauthorized data transfers, monitoring employee activity, and maintaining audit trails, insurers can reduce data leakage risks, simplify compliance audits, and avoid the visibility gaps created by multiple disconnected security tools.
